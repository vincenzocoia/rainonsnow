The mixture tracks the marginal for a while and then peels away toward
the conditional EVI. Where it peels away depends on n, and at the sample
sizes this project has, that happens inside the range of interest.

The transport removes the mixture's deficit wherever the conditional EVI
can be estimated at all. It fails at rho = 0.9 because CEVI = 0.19 there:
the conditional tail is nearly exponential, its index is hard to pin down,
and dividing by 0.19 multiplies whatever error remains by more than five.
