
=====================================================================
Copula transport against a generalized Pareto fitted to Y alone
=====================================================================

X ~ Pareto(4); Y | X = x ~ GP(scale = x, shape = xi). The marginal is
derived by quadrature and has asymptotic tail index 0.25 throughout, so
sweeping xi sweeps CEVI against a fixed target. Kernel bandwidth follows
one fixed rule and is never tuned. Paired samples, 60 replicates each.

=====================================================================

enough that its transported survival never reaches the target: the
estimator declines to extrapolate rather than guessing. That is a
property worth seeing, not a defect to hide, but it also means the rows
above are summarised over the replicates that did return something.
