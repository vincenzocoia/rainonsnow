Reading this table:
  'effective xi' is max_i(xi_i), the index the mixture actually inherits.
  'return level / truth' compares against the mixture built from the TRUE
  per-hour parameters, so 1.00x is exactly right.

Decomposition:
  Scale noise alone (oracle_shape, correct shape given): T=10y 1.01x   T=100y 1.07x   T=1000y 1.10x
  Any estimator's remaining gap from those numbers is its shape error.

  Note how `independent` looks respectable at T=10 and falls apart as T
  grows. That is not accuracy: its typical shape is biased low, which
  offsets the scale inflation, while max_i(xi_i) takes over further out.
  Two large errors cancelling at one return period is not a method.
