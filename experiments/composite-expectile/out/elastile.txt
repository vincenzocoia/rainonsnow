=== The alpha-elastile ===
At level p it is the root of

  G(t) = (2 alpha / s) [ (1 - 2p) phi(t) + (1 - p)(t - m) ] + (1 - alpha) [ F(t) - p ]

The first bracket vanishes at the expectile, the second at the quantile, and
both are increasing in t, so G is strictly increasing: the root is unique and
always lies between the quantile and the expectile. alpha = 0 gives the
quantile, alpha = 1 the expectile.

scale s (population convention) = 5.9027
