Note on the two references. The carried-back limiting GPD is NOT the truth's
conditional distribution, even as x grows: conditioning on X > u sweeps in the
body mass that the contamination adds at the threshold, so
   S_cond(x) / S_limit(x) -> 1 / (S_true(u) (1 + xi u / sigma)^(1/xi))
which here is 0.8629 (verified numerically to four decimals at x = 50, 200, 1000).
Equivalently, the GEV component carries only 0.8267 of the truth's survival at u.
So the limit is the right SHAPE to aim at, and the truth is the right LEVEL.
